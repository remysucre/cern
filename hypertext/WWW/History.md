WWW Project History

# History to date

A few steps to date in the WWW project history are as follows:

March 1989: First [project proposal](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/Proposal.md) written and circulated for comment[(TBL)](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#BernersLee) . Paper "HyperText and CERN" (in [ASCII](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/HTandCERN.txt) or [WriteNow](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/HTandCERN.wn) format) produced as background. October 1990: Project [proposal](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/Proposal.md) reformulated with encouragement from CN and ECP divisional management. [RC](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#Cailliau) is co-author. November 1990: Initial WorldWideWeb prototype developed on the NeXT[(TBL)](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#BernersLee) . November 1990: [Nicola Pellow](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#Pellow) joins and starts work on the line-mode browser . [Bernd Pollermann](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#Pollermann) helps get interface to CERNVM "FIND" index running. TBL gives a colloquium on hypertext in general. Christmas 1990: Line mode and NeXTStep browsers demonstrable. Acces is possible to hypertext files, CERNVM "FIND", and internet news articles. Febraury 1991: [workplan](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/WorkplanECP-PT.md) for the purposes of ECP division. 26 February 1991: [Presentation](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/Talks/Title.md) of the project to the ECP group. March 1991: Line mode browser (www) released to limited audience on priam vax, rs6000, sun4. May 1991: [Workplan](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/Administration/AS_WorkPlan_91.md) produced for CN/AS group 17 May 1991: [Presentation](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/Talks/C5_17_May_91.md) to C5 committee. General release of www on central CERN machines. 12 June 1991: CERN [Computer Seminar](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/Talks/ComputerSeminar.md) on WWW. August 1991: Files available on the net, posted on alt.hypertext (6, 16, 19th Aug), comp.sys.next (20th), comp.text.sgml and comp.mail.multi-media (22nd). [Jean-Francois Groff](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#Groff) joins the project. October 1991: VMS/HELP and WAIS gateways installed. Mailing lists www-interest (now www-announce) and www-talk@info.cern.ch started. One year status report. Anonymous telnet service started. December 1991: Presented poster and demonstration at [HT91](https://cern.la/hypertext/Conferences/HT91/Overview.md) . W3 browser installed on VM/CMS. CERN [computer newsletter](http://crnvmc/FIND?:cnl+204) announces W3 to the HEP world. 15 January 1992: Line mode browser release 1.1 available by anonymous FTP. See [news](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/News/9101.md) . Presentation to AIHEP'92 at La Londe. 12 February 1992: Line mode v 1.2 annouced on alt.hypertext, comp.infosystems, comp.mail.multi-media, cern.sting, comp.archives.admin, and mailing lists. May 1992: Presentation and [demo](https://cern.la/hypertext/Conferences/JENC92/Demonstrations.md#3) at [JENC3](https://cern.la/hypertext/Conferences/JENC92/Sessions.md) (Innsbruck). [Carl Barker](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/People.md#11) joins the project. June 1992: Presentation and demo at HEPVM (Lyon). July 1992: Distribution of WWW through CernLib, including Viola. WWW library code ported to DECnet. Report to the Advisory Board on Computing. August 1992: [introduction of CVS](https://cern.la/hypertext/WWW/IntroCVS.md) for code management.
